Privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026. This policy covers Edo to Now Studio ("the app"), a private tool used only by the owner of the Edo to Now TikTok account.

What the app accesses

When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives the permissions the owner approves: basic profile information (user.info.basic: an account identifier, display name and avatar) and permission to upload videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload).

How it is used

The app uses these permissions only to upload the channel's own videos to the owner's inbox. It does not read, collect or store information about other TikTok users, viewers, followers, comments or messages, and it does not publish anything: the owner reviews and posts each draft in the TikTok app.

Storage and sharing

The access tokens TikTok issues are stored encrypted as secrets in the channel's private build system and are used only by the upload job. No personal data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. The stored tokens are then useless and are deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message @edo.to.now on TikTok.
